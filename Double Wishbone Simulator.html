What the model includes, and what it simplifies
  • Each corner's linkage is solved exactly in the front (y–z) plane: arm lengths, pivots, upright, spindle, coilover mount. Camber, track, motion ratio and roll centre come from that solution at every travel position.
  • Dynamics: body heave, pitch and roll (small angles) plus vertical motion of each wheel. Tires are linear springs with light damping and can leave the ground.
  • No side-view geometry, so no anti-dive or anti-squat: all longitudinal load transfer goes through the springs.
  • Lateral load transfer through the links uses the symmetric-heave roll centre height at each axle's average travel. The live rear view shows the true asymmetric roll centre for reference.
  • Coil springs can't pull: they unseat at full droop. Bump stops and a droop limit act at the travel limits you set.
  • Anti-roll bar drop links are assumed adjusted for zero bar preload at the target heights.
  • No steering, caster, toe, or tire lateral forces; forces are applied as body accelerations.
